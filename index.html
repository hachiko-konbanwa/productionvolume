❓ How to use this map

🚀 Quick start

  1. Pick a crop from the panel (or type in the 🔍 Find a crop… box). The map colors in — darker means more production.
  2. Click a region to zoom in and see its provinces. Click a province for its details. Click the sea to zoom back out.
  3. Change the year (2021–2026) with the timeline at the bottom of the panel, or press ▶ to animate through the years.

⚠ 2026 is a partial year. PSA publishes an annual figure only once the fourth quarter closes, so 2026 shows just the part of the year reported so far — and how much that is varies by commodity. It will read far lower than 2021–2025 for reasons that have nothing to do with harvests, so don't compare it with a finished year or read a trend into it. For the periods that are complete, switch the timeline to Quarter or Semester, where Palay and Corn carry 2026 Q1, Q2 and 1st SEM.

📏 The three measurements

TabMeaning
VolumeHow much was produced (MT = metric tons, 1 MT = 1,000 kg)
YieldHow productive the land is (MT per hectare)
AreaHow much land was harvested (HA = hectares)
TreesNumber of bearing trees/hills (Mango and Banana only)

📚 Data source: Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), production years 2021–2026.

🎨 The colors

The legend explains them: Peak (darkest) = top 3 producers · High = above national average · Average / Low = at or below it · No Data = nothing reported.

💡 Hover a legend row to spotlight that class on the map; click to pin the highlight.

🪧 Reading the popup card

Click any region or province: you'll see its production, its % share, the change vs last year (green ▲ up / red ▼ down), its lowest, highest and average since 2021, and a mini trend chart — up-slope means growing; the blue dot is the year you're viewing.

📊 Get the numbers

Click 📊 View data table at the bottom of the legend for the full table (regions, or provinces when zoomed in). ⬇ Export CSV downloads it ready for Excel or Google Sheets.

🧭 The navigation rail (top-left)

The dark bar holds every tool: zoom, my location, measure, place search, fullscreen, this help, the crop panel toggle, and Layers — where you change the background map, terrain shading, boundaries and color opacity. Hover any icon to see its name.

When zoomed into a region, a breadcrumb appears above the timeline ("Philippines › Region …") — click it (or press Esc) to zoom back out. The year timeline floats at the bottom of the map.

💡 The web address updates as you explore — copy it to share exactly what you're looking at (crop, year, measurement, and region).

🏆 Top 10 Commodities

Click the "Top 10 Commodities ▼" button at the bottom of the map (or just scroll down) for a ranked catalogue of every crop by production volume — pick Philippines, an island group, a region, or a province, and optionally a Category. It follows whichever year is selected above. "▲ Back to map" returns you.

📋 View full ranking opens every ranked commodity in a table — search it, sort by Volume or Yield, or export it as CSV. ⇄ Compare areas shows two areas' top 5 side by side.

📱 On your phone

Tap the bar at the bottom to open the crop list — it closes by itself after you pick. Swipe the handle up/down to open/close. The legend (top-right) is fully open by default — tap ▼ to tuck it away.

🛟 Troubleshooting

  • "No data" — that area didn't report that crop that year. Not an error.
  • Negros Island Region missing before 2024? Correct — NIR was created in 2024; earlier years count its provinces under Western/Central Visayas.
  • Red error message? Check your internet and reload the page.

📊 Data coverage

These commodities have Volume only — PSA publishes no way to measure their productivity. Yield is volume ÷ area, so a crop with no Area cannot have a Yield either. Tree crops are not listed here: they carry Trees and Yield/Tree, which is the right measure for an orchard — use Show all to see them.
